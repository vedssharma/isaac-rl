- **Depth student, feed-forward**: CNN + MLP, distilled from the heading teacher. W&B runs: s42 `jziy8wep`, s1 `sfzu8umn`, s2 `nqbifc1z`
- **Depth student, GRU**: CNN + GRU, distilled from the teacher above (Phase 3 result). W&B runs: s42 `uke1cqz8`, s1 `x5bl74jf`, s2 `gik2yxvq`
- **Depth student, GRU, randomized**: trained under domain randomization (Phase 4 result). W&B runs: s42 `0g9bvtcl`, s1 `bmylysop`, s2 `jegy71of`

"L5 randomized": level 5 under full domain randomization (docs/phase4-robustness.md §2); the depth
policies also with depth noise × 2.
